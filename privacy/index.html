Privacy & analytics

Last updated November 21, 2026.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand website traffic and interactions, including guide views, project links, video interactions and outbound product links. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. Our event configuration avoids sending email addresses and full URL query strings.

Optional Meta (Facebook) Pixel

With your permission, we load Meta Pixel (ID 1487750289922224). It sends page-view activity and limited outbound affiliate-click events to Meta to help us understand the effectiveness of our Facebook traffic. Meta may use this data under its own policies. We do not enable automatic advanced matching. The Meta Pixel does not confirm whether you bought a product on a retailer's website.

Affiliate links

Some product links lead to third-party stores through Geniuslink or other affiliate services. We may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Those third parties have their own privacy practices.

Meta analytics choice

The Meta Pixel is disabled until you choose to allow it. Your preference is stored in your browser and can be changed here. If you decline, we do not load the Meta Pixel. This choice controls only Meta tracking, not Google Analytics or third-party links.
