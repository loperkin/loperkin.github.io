Workshop equipment

The documented setup uses a Bambu Lab A1 printer, BIQU CryoGrip Pro Glacier plate, Bambu PETG Basic and Translucent filament, and Bambu Studio. PETG is preferred for many functional prints because of toughness and heat resistance compared with standard PLA.

Print settings and troubleshooting

The workshop documents PETG and PLA starting profiles, adhesion, strength, cooling and common print problems. Settings are starting points; validate fit and durability for your own printer, filament and part.

Functional electronics enclosures

Projects include the wall-mounted Alarmo keypad case, original MultiSensor enclosures and the Decora-style sensor housing. These designs demonstrate how 3D printing can turn a working electronics prototype into a cleaner finished installation.

Browse printable project documentation →

Design, print, test, revise

The workshop emphasizes an iterative process: design a useful part, print it, test the fit, correct problems and print again. Other projects include household fixes, adapters, brackets and experiments.
