What this project adds

This is a companion to BrobstonCreations’ original Yet Another Smart Vent, not a claim of ownership of its mechanism. The additions modernize ESPHome configuration, offer Home Assistant HVAC automation examples and provide independently designed vent frames.

Original vent mechanism and assembly instructions →

ESPHome hardware and calibration

The documented configuration uses an ESP8266 D1 Mini, DFRobot DMS-MG90-A servo on D3, 50 Hz servo frequency, an approximately seven-second transition and servo detachment after movement. The author’s calibration is roughly 0% open and -80% closed; each build needs its own calibration.

Updated ESPHome firmware →

Custom 3D-printable vent frames

Additional 4×10 and 6×12 frame sizes were developed to cover existing wall damage and work with the upstream vent mechanism. These are companion frames, not replacements for the upstream louvers and gears.

Download the custom frames →

Home Assistant automation and safety

The repository includes room-level HVAC automation examples and sensor-failure fail-safe behavior. Changing vent openings can affect duct pressure and HVAC performance: follow the upstream project's static-pressure and temperature-differential recommendations and consult a qualified HVAC professional before widespread installation.
