Development status — revised design in progress

Important: initial testing found inaccurate temperature measurements because the DHT22 was too close to the presence sensor. The project author advises waiting before building or using the current version. A revised design is planned with an SHT45 positioned farther from the other electronics.

What the Decora MultiSensor is designed to do

This wall-mounted evolution of the original MultiSensor combines mmWave presence, ambient light, temperature and humidity in a Decora-style wall plate, with ESPHome and Home Assistant integration. The design is still being refined.

Electronics and firmware

The documented prototype uses an ESP32-C3 Super Mini, HLK-LD2410C, BH1750 and DHT22. The planned SHT45 revision means the current hardware list should not be treated as a finalized bill of materials.

Automation ideas and licensing

Once the revised hardware is validated, presence and light data can help automate lighting, while temperature and humidity support room monitoring. Software is listed as MIT and original functional hardware designs as CERN-OHL-W-2.0.
