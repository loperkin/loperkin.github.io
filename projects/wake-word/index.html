What is a wake word?

A wake word is a short phrase that tells a voice assistant to start listening for a request. It's the difference between a device waiting for its activation phrase and a device actively processing a spoken command. Wake-word detection is only one part of a complete voice assistant: recognizing the phrase, understanding the request, and performing an action are separate steps.

Why experiment with local wake-word detection?

Local detection can process the activation phrase on supported hardware without sending every sound to a remote service. That can reduce reliance on cloud connectivity for the wake-word stage, although other parts of a voice assistant may still need network access or external services. Performance depends on the microphone, room noise, model, and device resources.

What this project explores

This project area is for experimenting with wake-word models and voice interaction around a DIY smart home. Useful questions include whether a device reliably recognizes its activation phrase, how often background conversations trigger it by mistake, and how well it performs at different distances. A working end-to-end voice assistant also needs a way to handle commands after the wake word is detected.
