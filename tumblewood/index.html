Meet the Tumblewood family

Tumblewood is a family-friendly animated world built around everyday adventures, unexpected mishaps, and the small moments that make family life funny. The first story follows a fox family led by Finn, a dad raising three kids who each bring a different perspective to the adventure.

Finn

The dad trying to keep the family adventure on track, even when the kids' ideas take the day in a completely different direction.

Liv

Creative, expressive, and ready to turn an ordinary outing into something more imaginative.

Emmy

Thoughtful and analytical, often noticing details and looking for a practical way forward.

Johnny

Finn's son and an important part of the sibling dynamic, bringing his own energy to the family's plans.

What is Still Fishing about?

Still Fishing introduces the family through a fishing adventure. It's meant to be playful, easy to follow, and fun for kids and parents to watch together. You don't need to know anything about Tumblewood before watching the first episode.

How our family makes the show

Tumblewood is a family creative project, not a technology tutorial. The kids and I read the episode together in a family table read, which gives viewers a look at the voices, jokes, and timing behind the finished animated story. Watch the episode first, then compare it with the table read to see how the same story works in two different formats.

Where the series goes next

The idea is to build a larger community of animal families and tell more short, relatable stories about childhood, imagination, and family adventures. Future stories and characters will be introduced as episodes are completed; this page focuses on the first published episode and its behind-the-scenes recording.
